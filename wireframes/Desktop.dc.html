Description

A classic traveling step: step on one foot, shuffle the other, leap onto the shuffling foot, then tap the opposite toe behind. Usually repeated while moving sideways.

Practice Tips

Keep the leap light: a quick transfer of weight, not a big jump.
Make the final toe tap crisp, just behind the landing foot.
